Where to Apply for your MOF License Directly

To apply for official government catering tenders (meetings, conferences, events) exceeding RM20,000, you must register directly through Malaysia’s official public procurement portal:

Official Platform Name

ePerolehan Portal (eP)

Open ePerolehan Portal

Exact Navigation Path on Portal:

  1. Visit www.eperolehan.gov.my
  2. Navigate to 'New Supplier Registration'
  3. Select and submit under 'MOF Account' (Supplier Registration)

System vs. Tiers: ePerolehan vs. Akaun Asas vs. MOF Account

Understanding the distinct account structures and costs for first-time business owners.

System Gateway

ePerolehan System

The national electronic procurement marketplace where all government tenders, quotations, and direct purchases are published and processed.

Platform Type: Government Portal
Access Fee: Free to Browse
Basic Entry Tier

Akaun Asas

A free basic account for small vendors. Allows bidding only for direct purchases (Pembelian Terus) up to RM20,000. Not sufficient for major formal catering tenders.

Cost: RM 0 (Free)
Limit: ≤ RM20,000
Required for Tenders

MOF License Account

The full corporate supplier registration. Unlocks government quotations, department events, and formal tenders exceeding RM20,000.

Total Cost: RM 450
Validity: 3 Years

Frequently Asked Questions for First-Time Owners

Everything Koppiku needs to know about costs, validity, and catering requirements.

1. Where exactly do we apply for the MOF license?

You apply directly online through the official ePerolehan Portal (www.eperolehan.gov.my) under 'New Supplier Registration' -> 'MOF Account'.

2. What is the total cost and validity period of an MOF Account?

The total application fee is RM450 (comprising a RM50 non-refundable processing fee and a RM400 approval fee upon successful review). The issued Sijil MOF is valid for 3 years.

3. Can Koppiku skip the Basic Account (Akaun Asas) and apply straight for the MOF Account?

Yes! Since Koppiku wants to cater government department meetings, conferences, and formal tenders above RM20,000, you should register directly for the paid MOF Account rather than the free Basic Account.

4. Are there specific food and safety prerequisites for government catering?

Yes, government departments require valid local authority (PBT) business licenses, Ministry of Health hygiene compliances (like MeSTI), and ideally Halal certification status to ensure full compliance for official functions.
